How it works

Three steps to a clear read on your skin.

Clarity Labs is a free tool that looks at one photo of your skin, points out every blemish, and tells you what type it is, how bad it is overall, and what to do about it — in plain language.

01

Upload a photo

Drag in a clear, well-lit photo of the area you want analyzed. It's processed privately and never saved.

02

The model scans

Clarity Labs boxes each visible finding, labels it by type, and summarizes the overall activity without making a diagnosis.

03

Get your plan

Ask for advice and get a tailored routine plus product picks at every budget — so you know your next step.

What it detects

Six blemishes, told apart.

Most tools just count "spots." Clarity Labs distinguishes the actual types — because a whitehead and a nodule are not the same thing.
