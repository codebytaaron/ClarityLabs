The problem

Skin concerns create a lot of guesswork.

People can see that something changed, but they may not know what type of blemish they are looking at—or which products actually match it.

01

Hard to identify

Different visible findings can look similar without a trained model.

02

Advice feels generic

Most routines are disconnected from what is visible in the person’s photo.

03

Too many choices

Product aisles and online recommendations can leave users with no clear next step.

Talk track

“The issue is not a lack of information. It is turning a visible concern into guidance that feels relevant, understandable, and safe.”

Our solution

One scan. Three useful layers.

01 · MAP

Visible detections

Bounding boxes show where the model found each supported blemish class.

02 · SUMMARIZE

Activity overview

Counts and weighted findings create a neutral, non-diagnostic visual summary.

03 · GUIDE

Personalized plan

The image, detections, and user profile shape a routine and product options by budget.

Talk track

“We are not replacing a dermatologist. We are making the first step clearer by showing what the model sees and connecting it to practical education.”

How it works

Two models do different jobs.

Input

A clear photo

The browser resizes the image so both models analyze the same pixels efficiently.

Roboflow

Detect and count

A YOLO object-detection model returns classes, locations, and confidence values.

Groq Vision

Explain and tailor

The vision model combines the photo, detections, and user preferences into readable guidance.

Talk track

“Roboflow gives us structure and precision. Groq adds visual context and communicates the result in language personalized to the user.”

Safety and scope

Helpful by design. Not a diagnosis.

Clarity Labs is an educational first-look tool. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medication, or replace licensed care.

Neutral languageResults describe visible activity instead of assigning a medical severity rating.
Clear limitsThe plan acknowledges lighting, framing, and uncertainty when they affect the scan.
Escalation guidancePersistent, painful, or worsening concerns should be discussed with a licensed dermatologist.
